◈ HOW TANNIS PROTECTS YOU
◈ HOW TO RUN TANNIS ▼
CHOOSING YOUR MODE — WELCOME SCREEN
When you open TANNIS you'll see three options:

■ PURE LOCAL — 100% air-gapped. Nothing ever leaves your machine. No cloud AI, no wake word, no online features. Maximum privacy.

◆ TANNIS — Your last saved settings. If you've used TANNIS before this restores exactly where you left off — model choice, online/offline state, everything.

⚙ MY OWN API — Bring your own Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini API key. Billed directly by your provider. Your key never touches our servers.
STATUS BAR — TOP OF SCREEN
The status bar shows you exactly what TANNIS is doing at all times:

OFFLINE — using local AI, nothing is sent anywhere
RESEARCH — online mode active, real-time AI and web search
PURE LOCAL — fully air-gapped, zero external connections
OWN KEY — using your personal API key

The right side shows memory status, voice engine, and active indicators for SPEAKING, GENERATING, LOOP ACTIVE, and INCOGNITO when relevant.
SETTINGS — ⚙ BUTTON
AI MODE — Switch between Offline and My Own AI. In Offline mode you can toggle Pure Local (zero internet) or Real-Time Research Mode (connects to Claude for smarter responses and live web search).

OFFLINE MODEL — Choose which local AI model to use. FAST is quickest for simple questions. STANDARD is the recommended default. CODER is specialized for code. POWER is the strongest but needs a GPU to feel fast.

MIC CONTROL — Configure wake word detection, back-and-forth conversation loop, stop phrases, and silence timeout.

VOICE — Enable TANNIS to speak responses. Choose between ElevenLabs (best quality), OpenAI TTS (high quality), or System voice (100% local, no internet).

APPEARANCE — Theme presets, custom colors, font choice, and font size.

MEMORY — Control how TANNIS remembers you across sessions. Choose Light, Balanced, or Full memory mode. Set extraction frequency, auto-forget threshold, or trigger a manual extraction.
ABOUT YOU — ☺ BUTTON
Your profile helps TANNIS personalise conversations. There are two separate profiles:

OFFLINE PROFILE — stored only on this device, never sent anywhere. Fill this in freely — name, occupation, goals, interests, location, communication style.

ONLINE PROFILE — a separate profile used only when online mode is active and sharing is OFF. Fill in only what you're comfortable sending to an external server.

The SHARE MY PROFILE WITH ONLINE AI toggle lets your local offline profile be used by the cloud AI directly — no need to fill in a separate online profile.
MIC AND VOICE
Click the 🎤 mic button to start recording. TANNIS uses Whisper to transcribe your voice locally — your audio never leaves your device. It auto-sends after a short silence.

The X button that appears on the mic cancels the recording without sending.

WAKE WORD — Say your assistant's name at any time to open the mic hands-free. Configure poll speed in Settings to balance CPU usage vs responsiveness.

BACK & FORTH LOOP — After TANNIS replies, the mic reopens automatically for a continuous hands-free conversation. Say a stop phrase or click the LOOP ACTIVE X to end it.
HOW MEMORY WORKS
TANNIS builds a memory of you across sessions automatically. There are two layers:

KEYWORD SCAN — instant detection of things like your name, location, job, or goals from what you type. Happens every message with no delay.

AI EXTRACTION — every few messages TANNIS runs a full analysis of recent conversation and extracts facts into your profile. You'll see a ◈ MEMORY UPDATED notification when this happens.

Memory is stored separately for offline and online modes — your local memory never goes to the cloud and vice versa. Clear it anytime in Settings → MEMORY.
CHAT HISTORY — ☰ BUTTON
Every conversation is automatically saved. Click ☰ to see recent chats or VIEW ALL HISTORY for the full list. Click any session to restore it. The + button starts a fresh new chat.

Incognito mode prevents any messages from being saved to history at all.

POP OUT opens it in a separate floating window with its own mic, loop, and stop controls.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE — AND WHEN
TYPING
Your message text is sent the moment you press SEND — not while you type. In offline mode it never leaves. In online mode it travels to our secure proxy server, then to Anthropic. It is never stored on our server.
VOICE
Voice input (mic) is processed 100% locally using Whisper — your audio never leaves your device. Voice output (TTS) using ElevenLabs or OpenAI does send text to their servers to generate audio. System voice is fully local.
MEMORY
Memory summaries are generated every 5 messages in online mode. A short summary of your conversation — not your full chat — is sent to Claude to build context. This summary is saved locally on your device only.
PROFILE
Your offline profile is stored only on this device and never sent anywhere by default. If you enable "Share profile with online AI," your profile is included in requests to help personalize responses.

◉ HOW INCOGNITO MODE WORKS
When Incognito is ON — no messages are saved to your chat history, no memory updates are written to disk, and no profile changes are saved. The moment you turn it off, normal saving resumes. Your current session's messages in incognito are gone when you close TANNIS.

Incognito does NOT prevent your messages from being sent to servers in online mode — it only controls what gets saved locally on your device.
◈ HOW WE PROTECT YOUR API ACCESS
TANNIS never stores raw API keys on your device for the online research mode. Instead all cloud AI requests go through our secure proxy server. Your messages travel encrypted over HTTPS, our server makes the API call, and the response is returned to you. Our server does not log or store your messages.

If you use "My Own API" mode, your personal API key is stored locally on your device only and sent directly to your chosen provider — it never touches our servers.

📄 DOCUMENTS HUB
The Documents Hub is a built-in notepad that lives entirely on your device. You can create, edit, and save documents directly inside TANNIS — and ask TANNIS to write into them for you.

MY DOCUMENTS — documents you create manually. Stored locally on the drive, never uploaded anywhere.

CHAT DOCUMENTS — documents created from a conversation (e.g. asking TANNIS to draft something). Also stored locally only.

Documents can be exported as HTML files to your computer. No document content is ever sent to any server unless you copy it into a message in online mode yourself.
◆ PROJECTS
Projects let you organise separate workspaces inside TANNIS — each with its own name, description, notes, response style, chat history, and memory. Useful for keeping work, personal, and creative conversations completely separate.

What gets stored per project (all local on your device):
■ Project name and description
■ Project notes — context you write for TANNIS to always remember in this project
■ A custom response style override for this project
■ Separate chat history and memory, isolated from your general chat

Important: Your project notes and project memory are automatically injected into every message you send inside that project. This means if you are in online or research mode, that project context travels to the AI server along with your message. If your project contains sensitive information, use it in Offline or Pure Local mode only.

Deleting a project removes all its data from your device permanently.
💻 CODE MODE
Code Mode connects the Documents Hub to your conversation. When enabled, TANNIS automatically reads the contents of your open document so you don't have to paste your code into every message.

The document content is included in your message context — so if you are in online mode, that code will be sent to the AI server along with your message. If you are in offline or pure local mode, it never leaves your device.

Tip: if your code is sensitive, use Code Mode in Offline or Pure Local mode only.
↑ OTA UPDATES — OVER THE AIR
TANNIS checks for updates automatically on launch by contacting GitHub to see if a newer version exists. If one is found, you are asked whether to install it — it is never applied without your approval.

What happens during an update:
■ A zip file is downloaded from the official Titan Technologies GitHub release page
■ It is extracted and overwrites only the app files — not your config, memory, profile, or chat history
■ TANNIS restarts automatically once the update is installed

Your personal data, settings, and conversation history are never touched by an update. Updates only affect the app code itself. If you are offline or decline the update, nothing changes.
📝 LOCAL UPDATE LOGGER
TANNIS keeps a local log file stored at ~/.tannis/logs/tannis.log on your computer. This log records technical events to help diagnose issues if something goes wrong.

What gets logged:
■ App start and shutdown events
■ Your OS, CPU, and RAM at launch (for diagnostics only)
■ TANNIS version and update installs
■ License check results (pass/fail — not your key itself)
■ Ollama and Whisper start/stop events
■ Any errors that occur

What is never logged:
■ Your messages or conversations
■ Your memory or profile
■ Your API keys

This log file stays on your machine only and is never transmitted anywhere. You can view or delete it at any time by navigating to the path above.
〜 VISUALIZER
The Visualizer is an animated display that reacts to TANNIS speaking, listening, and idle states in real time. Choose between three designs — WAVEFORM, RADIAL, and DOT SPHERE — using the arrow buttons.

POP OUT — opens the visualizer in a separate floating window with its own mic toggle, loop toggle, and stop controls, so you can move it anywhere on screen while you work.

The visualizer only reads the current state of the app — speaking, listening, or idle. It does not record audio, capture your messages, or send anything anywhere. It is purely a local visual display.
☺ ABOUT ME — YOUR PROFILE
The About Me screen lets you build a personal profile so TANNIS can personalise every conversation. There are two completely separate profiles:

OFFLINE PROFILE — stored only on this drive, never sent anywhere under any circumstances. Fill this in freely with your name, occupation, goals, interests, location, and communication style. This is used by the local AI only.

ONLINE PROFILE — a separate, minimal profile used only when online mode is active and the share toggle is OFF. Only fill in what you are comfortable sending to an external server. Leave any field blank to keep it private.

SHARE MY PROFILE WITH ONLINE AI — when this toggle is ON, your offline profile is sent to the cloud AI instead of the online profile. This gives Claude better personalisation but means Anthropic can see those details. Keep it OFF if your offline profile contains anything sensitive.

Your profile is never used for advertising, sold to third parties, or stored on our servers. It lives entirely on this drive.
🎤 WAKE WORD & CONVERSATION LOOP — DATA & PRIVACY
WAKE WORD — When enabled, TANNIS periodically listens for your assistant's name using Whisper running locally on your device. No audio is ever recorded or uploaded during this listening phase — only the keyword match result is used. Wake word detection is automatically disabled in Pure Local mode.

BACK & FORTH LOOP — After TANNIS responds, the mic reopens automatically so you can keep talking hands-free. Each voice input is still transcribed locally by Whisper. The resulting text follows the same rules as a typed message — it stays local in offline mode and goes through the secure proxy in online mode.

Neither wake word nor loop audio is ever stored to disk or sent anywhere. Only the final text transcript is used.
◈ ERROR REPORTING — SENTRY
TEMPORARY · REMOVED IN UPDATE 1
TANNIS currently uses a service called Sentry to automatically collect crash reports and technical error logs. This helps the Titan Technologies team identify and fix bugs before they affect more users.

What Sentry can see:
■ App crashes and error stack traces
■ Which part of the app caused the error
■ Your operating system and app version

What Sentry cannot see:
■ Your messages or conversations
■ Your memory or profile data
■ Your API keys or license key
■ Any files you attach or documents you write

Sentry will be fully removed in TANNIS Update 1. Once that update is installed, no data of any kind leaves your device unless you explicitly choose an online feature. We are disclosing this now because we believe you have the right to know exactly what runs on your machine.
FOLLOW THE DATA — PLAIN ENGLISH
WHEN YOU SEND A MESSAGE IN RESEARCH MODE
Your message travels from your device → our secure server → Anthropic's Claude. Anthropic can see your questions. They do not store them for training by default, but their privacy policy applies. This is why we recommend starting a new chat when switching topics — especially personal ones. Your full chat history is never sent all at once, only the recent context window.
WHEN TANNIS REMEMBERS THINGS
Every 5 messages in online mode, TANNIS sends a short summary request to Claude asking it to extract key facts from your recent conversation. Claude sees those recent messages to build the summary. The summary itself is saved only on your device — never on our server. If you don't want this, turn on Incognito mode and nothing gets saved at all.
WHEN YOU SHARE YOUR PROFILE WITH ONLINE AI
If you turn on "Share profile with online AI," your name, occupation, goals, and other profile fields are included at the start of every message sent to Claude. This helps Claude personalize responses but means Anthropic can see that info. If this makes you uncomfortable, keep it off and fill in the separate Online Profile with only what you're comfortable sharing.
WHEN YOU USE ELEVENLABS OR OPENAI VOICE
When TANNIS speaks a response using ElevenLabs or OpenAI TTS, the text of that response is sent to their servers to generate audio. They can see what TANNIS said to you. If this concerns you, switch to System voice in Settings — it runs 100% on your device and sounds great on Windows and Mac.
WHEN YOU USE YOUR MIC
Your voice is recorded locally and processed by Whisper — a local AI model running entirely on your device. Your audio is never uploaded anywhere. The transcript it produces is then treated like a typed message and follows the same rules as above depending on which mode you're in.
WHEN YOU USE OFFLINE OR PURE LOCAL MODE
Nothing leaves your device. Ever. Ollama runs the AI model locally on your hardware. Your messages, profile, memory, and history all stay on your machine. The tradeoff is that local models are less powerful than Claude and don't have access to real-time information.
OUR RECOMMENDATION
Use offline mode for personal conversations, health topics, finances, or anything sensitive. Switch to Research mode when you need real-time information or more powerful AI. Start a new chat when switching between sensitive and non-sensitive topics. Enable Incognito when you want zero local traces.
